Value creation from local resources

Making More of What We Have

Potential that was once seen as ordinary material or left unused is now being recognized as a source of value. Groups process local resources, including by-products and waste, into marketable products.

Through a circular economy approach, resources that would otherwise go to waste can be repurposed to generate economic value. This creates opportunities for groups to innovate, develop new products, and expand their business prospects.
