Collective Effort, Sustainable Livelihoods

For women’s groups, businesses that were once run informally are now becoming more structured and collectively managed.

Clear roles, financial record-keeping, production management, and regular evaluations help members develop their businesses with greater confidence. Collective business management also helps ensure greater continuity and reduces reliance on just one or two individuals.
