More informed farm management

Know your land, plan what’s next.

Mapping gives farmers a clearer picture of their farms; from boundaries and land zones to surrounding landscapes and forest functions. Using tools such as Avenza Maps, farmers can conduct basic mapping themselves.

In the long run, this effort will support better decisions on farm management, land use, and future planning, while providing a foundation for accessing government support and developing collective initiatives such as cooperatives, farmer entities, or associations.
