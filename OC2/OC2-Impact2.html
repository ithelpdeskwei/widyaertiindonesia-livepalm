Clear land boundaries and zoning awareness

Defined zones, better land management.

Before mapping, farmers could only estimate their land boundaries based on their own knowledge and physical markers in the field. After mapping, they have a clearer understanding of their farm boundaries, allowing them to manage and protect their land more effectively.

Mapping also helps farmers understand land zonation. By knowing the different zones, farmers can identify which areas can be utilized and managed, and which areas fall within restricted or “red” zones.
