Clear land boundaries and zoning awareness

Defined zones, better land management.

Before mapping, farmers relied on personal knowledge and physical markers to estimate their land boundaries. Mapping provides a clearer picture of farm boundaries, helping farmers manage and protect their land more effectively.

It also helps farmers identify land-use zones, distinguishing areas available for cultivation from restricted or “red” zones.
