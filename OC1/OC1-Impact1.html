Improved Farming

Know better, Do better

Through hands-on training and Farmer Field School, farmers strengthen their understanding of Good Agricultural Practices (GAP) and regenerative agriculture.

The training focuses on practical farm management, including soil and nutrient management, cover crops, integrated pest management, and responsible use of farm inputs.
