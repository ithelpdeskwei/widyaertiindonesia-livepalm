Strengthening Farmer Organizations and Governance

A space to learn, organize, and grow

Through farmer groups, smallholders who once worked independently now have a space to learn, coordinate, support one another, and work towards shared goals.

As these groups become more organized, farm management and production activities become better coordinated. Clear roles, governance structures, and internal systems help groups operate more effectively.
