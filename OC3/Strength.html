Strengthening Farmer Organization
Management and Services

Certification is not the end goal. For LIVE PALM, the journey towards certification is an opportunity to build stronger farmer organizations that deliver meaningful benefits to their members.

Stronger Farmer Organizations

Clear structures, roles, and responsibilities enable farmers to work collectively, make decisions, and address the needs of their members.

Management for Sustainability

Knowledge, skills, and management systems help farmer organizations operate more effectively and accountably while supporting sustainable farming practices.

From Certification to Sustainability

Understanding ISPO and RSPO standards is part of building awareness and adopting sustainable practices, rather than just a set of compliances

Services for
Members

Stronger farmer organizations are equipped to provide relevant services that address members’ needs, connect them with support, and help them grow together.

Beyond Certification

Making Sustainability Part of Everyday Practice.

For farmers, certification is a starting point, not the finish line. By integrating sustainability standards into everyday farm management and organizational practices, farmer groups can make sustainability a shared and lasting commitment.
